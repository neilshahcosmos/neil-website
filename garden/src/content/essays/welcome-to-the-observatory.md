Most personal websites are built like a newspaper: finished articles, newest first, never
touched again once published. That format is good for news and bad for thinking. Ideas
don't arrive finished. They start as a vague hunch, gather material, collapse into
something coherent, and only occasionally become something worth defending.

So this site is built like a **digital garden** instead: a collection of pages at every stage of
that process, linked to each other, and revisited as they grow.

## The life cycle of an idea

Every page carries a label borrowed from [[growth-stages-as-star-formation|star formation]]:

- **Nebula**: a loose cloud of an idea. Rough, incomplete, possibly wrong.
- **Protostar**: collapsing into shape. Mostly coherent, still gathering material.
- **Star**: mature and stable. I'd stand behind it.

The label tells you how much weight to put on what you're reading, and gives me permission
to publish things before they're perfect.

## What lives here

The [math notes](/notes/) are the oldest and most carefully tended part of the garden: worked
notes on algebra, Lie theory and quantum field theory, such as
[[notes:lie-theory/su2-covers-so3]]. Around them grow [essays](/essays/) like this one, shorter
[thoughts](/thoughts/) like [[symmetry-before-sets]], [projects and games](/projects/), and
[film reviews](/reviews/).

## How to wander

Follow your curiosity. Hover over a link to preview where it goes. At the bottom of each page
there's a list of the other pages that link to it, so you can walk the garden backwards as well
as forwards. Topics group related pages into constellations, and [the garden](/garden/) shows
everything at once.
