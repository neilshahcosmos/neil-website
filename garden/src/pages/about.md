Hello! I'm Neil. I'm working my way through the mathematics behind modern physics, 
starting with Abstract Algebra, then Lie Groups, then onwards towards Quantum Field Theory, 
and writing down what I learn as I go.

This site is my **digital garden**: a place for ideas at every stage of growth rather than 
a feed of polished posts. You'll find:

- **[Math notes](/notes/)** : carreful, worked notes on Abstract Algebra, Lie Theory, and QFT
- **[Essays](/essays/)** : longer arguments and shorter musings
