I'm Neil Shah. I'm working my way through the mathematics behind modern physics, starting
with abstract algebra, then Lie groups and their representations, and on towards quantum
field theory, and writing down what I learn as I go.

This site is my **digital garden**: a place for ideas at every stage of growth rather than a
feed of polished posts. You'll find:

- **[Math notes](/notes/)**: careful, worked notes on algebra, Lie theory, set theory and QFT,
  plus a longer [book](/notes/book/) that collects the polished material.
- **[Essays](/essays/)** and **[thoughts](/thoughts/)**: longer arguments and shorter musings.
- **[Projects & games](/projects/)**: things I've built.
- **[Film reviews](/reviews/)**: what I've been watching.

Each page carries a growth stage (nebula, protostar or star) so you know how much weight
to put on it. Pages link to each other, and each one lists the pages that link back to it,
so the best way to explore is to follow your curiosity.
